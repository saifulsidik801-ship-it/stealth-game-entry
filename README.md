**Ember Market — Lantern Thief.** A top-down 2D stealth game: sneak through a night market, hack the ember lanterns, reach the gate unseen. Wardens patrol with lantern-light vision cones that stalls and walls genuinely block; hide in shadow cover, sneak silent with SHIFT, throw decoys to lure wardens away. A suspicion ladder ("?" then "!") with a real recovery window — break line of sight and the alarm drains. Three levels, WebAudio footsteps that tell you where a guard is.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD move, SHIFT sneak, hold E to hack, SPACE / click to throw a decoy.
